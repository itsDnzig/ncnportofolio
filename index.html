01 / About Rahmat Firdaus® / Jakarta

It’s not just about creating art.
It’s about building emotion through light, shape, and motion.

Rahmat Firdaus is a Jakarta-based 3D designer, motion artist, and video editor. His approach is editorial: remove what is unnecessary, protect the rhythm, and let form speak.

He works end-to-end, from look development and animation to final grade, with national television, creative agencies, and independent studios.
